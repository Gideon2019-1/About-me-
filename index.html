01 / About

Research grounded in lived experience.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Technical Communication and Rhetoric at Texas Tech University, with an M.A. in Rhetoric and Professional Communication from New Mexico State University.

My interests include multimodal environmental activism, culturally responsive UX, AI in writing and content development, and environmental health risk communication. Across these areas, I ask whose needs are prioritized when information is designed—and whose perspectives may be left out.

02 / How I work

My research and design philosophy

Useful communication begins with understanding the people, contexts, and consequences involved.

Listen before designing

Center users' language, expectations, experiences, and constraints rather than assuming a universal audience.

Make information usable

Prioritize clear structure, accessible interactions, and meaningful pathways through complex content.

Keep human judgment visible

Use technology critically. Verify claims, acknowledge sources, and preserve context that automated summaries may flatten.

04 / Teaching

Helping writers make intentional choices.

I approach teaching as an iterative, user-centered practice.

As a writing instructor, I encourage students to consider how their communication choices affect real audiences. I value transparent expectations, constructive feedback, and opportunities to revise. I am especially interested in how digital tools—including AI—can support learning without replacing students' critical judgment.

Teaching areas: First-year composition, research writing, and professional communication.
