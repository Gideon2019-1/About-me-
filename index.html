About Me

I am a doctoral student at Texas Tech University with an academic background in rhetoric, professional communication, and writing instruction. My interests connect technical communication, user experience, information design, and socially responsible technology.

Through teaching and research, I am interested in making complex information more accessible and meaningful for diverse audiences.

Research Interests

My research explores how communication and design shape people's access to information and participation in decisions.

  • User experience (UX) and user-centered design
  • Technical and professional communication
  • Information and document design
  • Community-centered and participatory design
  • Environmental and public health communication
